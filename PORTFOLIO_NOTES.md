## Architecture & Design

### 1. Aesthetic Tuning :: Colony (Oct 2019)
- Collage-drawing experiments for Art Prison Competition 2017
- Procedural Rollage technique: superimposing images to de-contextualize content
- Explores "Aesthetic Drivers" as convergences of Organization::Qualities::Tectonics
- Viscous::Deep::Phasing Scape — Mediterranean Sea proximity, geological depth, jellyfish phasing
- Outputs: macro-level site organization, soft clustering volumes, flowing meandering paths
- Blog: https://www.blog.digitalnouveau.com/post/aesthetic-tuning-colony

### 2. THICK SPACE :: Mapping Ambiguous Territories (Aug 2019)
- Studio prompt for ARCH 201 :: Intermediate Design I at Pratt (Fall 2019)
- Focus: plan and mat building typology
- Walls as mediators/thresholds — destabilizing devices of interface and transition
- Collage-derived aesthetic drivers informing programmatic design
- Blog: https://www.blog.digitalnouveau.com/post/thick-space-mapping-ambiguous-territories

### 3. Learning to Design @ Home :: Drawing and Modeling (Jul 2020)
- Fall '19 / Spring '20 Pratt course for NYC high school students (co-taught with Olivia Vien)

- 2D abstract drawing translated to 3D form via cutting and folding sheet material

## Fabrication & Making

### 1. Hyphae Simulations (mk1, mk2, mk3) — Aug 2019
- Maya nParticles and dynamics simulating mycelium/filament growth
- 3 versions exploring increasingly complex rhizomatic networks
- Particles cross, entangle, merge into rhyzomatic connections
- Interested in organizational voids within the mesh, mixing multiple layers
- Future plans: digital-to-physical translation, particles as material, tectonic systems
- Blog: https://www.blog.digitalnouveau.com/post/hyphae-simulations-mk1

### 2. Landforms - Craggs (May 2014)
- Maya nParticles and dynamic fields for turbulent landscape deformation
- Particle grid linked by woven spring system — explicit control vs. emergent behavior
- Final goal: 3D printed landscape with embedded fossil objects from Arnica jewelry
- Design philosophy: establish broad objectives, rigorously test control parameters
- Blog: https://www.blog.digitalnouveau.com/post/landforms-craggs

### 3. Luna Moth Cocooning Ring — Sep/Oct 2014
- Custom ring design transforming Luna Moth pendant geometry into new life-cycle form
- Ring in "state of change, the cocooning" — insect symmetry, open wing patterns compressed into knot
- High-density internal spaces for light refraction in polished sterling silver
- Mexican fire opal gem setting
- 3D printed prototypes for fit, weight, balance testing; resin and nylon iterations
- Blog: https://www.blog.digitalnouveau.com/post/luna-moth-cocooning-ring-prototype-v01

### 4. Cocooning Ring Prototypes — Oct 2014
- Resin and Nylon prototype iterations
- Form and detail comparison, opal location and fit testing
- Design refinement: slimmer, more elegant form; tighter opening for flexible fit
- Blog: https://www.blog.digitalnouveau.com/post/cocooning-ring-prototypes

## Design Philosophy Notes (from Landforms post)
"Part of our design philosophy at Miscellaneous Projects is to establish broad objectives and then rigorously test control parameters to generate sets of possible trajectories. From these sets we can then narrow the search and test more specific parameters. This helps us to engage with the uncertainty of design and discover unexpected possibilities in the process."
