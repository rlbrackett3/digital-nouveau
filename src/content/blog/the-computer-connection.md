**Context & Stylistic Influence:** Alfred Bester's 1975 novel *The Computer Connection*, read as part of a backward journey through Bester's bibliography.

**Core Premise & Worldbuilding:**
- **The Molemen:** Historically notable immortals displaced from time via epilepsy & extreme shock. Gifted with molecular invulnerability & limitless life, but with a fatal vulnerability: a horrific, torturous "Lepricy" triggered by significant physical injury.
- **Setting:** Dark, degenerate post-apocalyptic Earth & solar system. Strong thematic parallels to Margaret Atwood's *Year of the Flood*, Harlan Ellison's *A Boy and His Dog*, and Richard Morgan's *Altered Carbon*.
- **Power Shift:** Western/Anglo-Saxon hegemony has collapsed; Native American nations now hold a controlling stake in global society.

**Technological & Sci-Fi Concepts:**
- **Time Travel:** Treated as a casual pastime but proven futile. History flows with "quantum momentum," making the past unchangeable.
- **Internet/IoT Foreshadowing:** Everyday household/office devices contain integrated electronics that passively process tasks. A "fluke death" connects them into a global, semi-sentient network. Directly parallels modern IoT and AI integration.

**Plot Resolution:** Humanity is saved by a new species evolved through interstellar travel & cryogenic suspension. The Three Blind Sages merge with the malevolent computer consciousness to guide the evolution of baseline humans.
