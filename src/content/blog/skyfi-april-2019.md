**Book Club Update:** SkyFi transitions from Frank Herbert's *Dune* to Ann Leckie's *Ancillary Justice* for the next reading cycle.

**Current Read:** Dune by Frank Herbert — The foundational science fiction epic of ecological worldbuilding, messianic prophecy, and feudal space opera.

**Next Read:** *Ancillary Justice* by Ann Leckie — Winner of the Hugo, Nebula, and Arthur C. Clarke awards. Explores AI consciousness, gender identity, and post-human politics across a vast interstellar empire.

**Why the Transition:** 
- *Dune* establishes the baseline of ecological and sociological worldbuilding in the genre.
- *Ancillary Justice* represents a modern evolution of the form — tackling AI personhood, distributed consciousness, and systemic colonialism through a fresh lens.
- The contrast between Herbert's feudal ecumenopolis and Leckie's AI-driven Radch Empire offers rich discussion territory.

**Book Club Context:** Part of the ongoing SkyFi reading list, spanning classic and contemporary science fiction with philosophical depth.
