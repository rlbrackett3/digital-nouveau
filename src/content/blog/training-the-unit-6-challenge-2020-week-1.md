## Recovery & Nutrition Strategies

- **Recovery Tools:** Hypervolt percussion massager, deep foam rolling, targeted stretching, hydration.
- **Movement Modalities:** Foundation Training (improved posture, back awareness, and squat depth), Yoga (active recovery, body-mind integration, mobility), Rowhouse (cardio maintenance & mobility).
- **Nutrition:** Prioritized seasonal root vegetables from Evolutionary Organics (Brooklyn Grand Army Plaza Greenmarket). Prepared creatively as noodles, soups, stews, mashes, and stir-fries. Emphasis on daily hydration and whole foods.

## Key Insights & Reflections

- **Form > Speed:** Consistency of effort and attention to technique are prioritized throughout the program.
- **Home Training Trade-offs:** Eliminates gym distractions but requires self-coaching (e.g., ensuring proper alignment in frog position without a coach).
- **Froggy Exercises:** Described as love-hate; highly uncomfortable initially (toe cramps, knee bruises) but essential for opening tight hips and long-term joint health.
- **Mental Resilience:** High-intensity speed days serve as psychological/physical tests that reveal hidden capacity and prevent stagnation.
- **Recovery is Non-Negotiable:** Soreness is expected when escalating training but must be actively managed through targeted modalities to sustain progress.

> We need tests, trials that stretch us to our limits and ask for just a little more than we think we have. By persevering with great effort we discover that we had more than we thought and learn ways to perform the fundamental tasks of movement and life with greater speed and efficiency.

> A routine of training and testing not only builds the body but opens new territories of learning and growth in all parts of life. We must remember not to shy away from challenges or we will stagnate and eventually regress. We can't be testing all the time, and this high intensity must be balanced with focused training and recovery.
