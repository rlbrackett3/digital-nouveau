**Format:** Double feature reading session covering two China Mieville collections: *Exploded View* and *New Dark Age*.

**Exploded View:** A curated collection of Mieville's earlier short stories, originally published across magazines like *Asimov's*, *Interzone*, *Weird Tales*, and *Alfred Hitchcock's Mystery Magazine*. A "sampler platter" of his storytelling range.

**New Dark Age:** Set in a far-future world post-civilizational collapse where technology from the Old Time has been entirely lost and mythologized as magic. Explores human nature and the cyclical rise/fall of societies. The collection is described as "a bit of a rollercoaster" — uneven but with exceptional highs.

> "The collection is a bit of a rollercoaster, but the highs are just so freaking high."

> "China Mieville is the greatest SF author writing today. He may be the only SF author writing today who writes like China Mieville. His unique ability to write in multiple genres, blend horror and fantasy and science fiction, and weave a complex narrative tapestry is unmatched."
