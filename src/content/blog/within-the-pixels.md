It's like the electric surface of a pool on a sunny day.

After Yesterday's post I kept playing around with the images of fungus (or molds, moss, lichen...) and just for fun threw an inversion filter into the stack. This gave me some great new insights into adjusting the color and lighting to really bring out the resolution and hyper reality of my phone's crazy camera. There is also the added bonus of two really amazing images that tell a whole new story about this already fascinating micro world of color, form, growth and life.
