**Purpose:** Pre-holiday book club check-in to evaluate progress on *Ninefox Gambit* by Yoon Ha Lee and select the next group read.

**Worldbuilding:** Centers on a mathematical calendar system that governs all technology, including interstellar travel and devastating weaponry.

**Social Structure:** Features an extremely rigid, unrelenting, and grotesque social order that discards human life at the slightest threat to systemic order.

**Author's Creative Process:** Yoon Ha Lee approaches fiction like mathematical argumentation, focusing on axioms, logical steps, and a final "theorem" or moral conclusion for the reader.

> "I approach writing like it's an equation. What is the… moral is maybe too loaded a term… but what is the thing at the end that the reader should come away with? What is the final conclusion? What is the theorem that I am trying to prove, and what are the axioms that will get me there, and how do I show the steps?"

**Next Book Considerations:**
- Jeff VanderMeer's *Dead Astronauts*
- William Gibson's *The Peripheral*

Stay the course with *Ninefox Gambit* and continue through the *Machineries of Empire* trilogy.
