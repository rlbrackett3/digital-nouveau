**Core Premise:** A scientist discovers a new form of energy — **Strange Stars** — capable of powering an interstellar ark to escape a dying Earth. She becomes convinced this phenomenon is **the physical presence of God**.

**Central Theme — Faith:** 
- The author explores belief as something **unquestioned and untested**, rather than something to be proven.
- Belief as **surrender to the unknowable** rather than reliance on empirical evidence.
- Contrasts religious certainty with scientific methodology — each valid in their own domain.

**Key Quotes & Reflections:**
> "A belief, a faith, a belief that you never question, you never test... It's not about the God of the Universe. It's about the God of the unknown."

> "I can't believe in the God of the universe, but I can believe in the God of the unknown."

**Group Discussion Points:**
- Author's intent to examine faith **without passing judgment** on whether it's "real."
- The scientist's **incredible leap of faith** parallels religious conversion experiences.
- Tension between **mystical intuition** and **methodical reasoning**.

**Author's Approach:** Intentionally avoids moralizing. Presents the scientist's journey without dictating whether her faith is justified, leaving readers to wrestle with the tension themselves.

> "I think one of the things I wanted to do with this book was to try to write from a perspective of, not of whether faith is real or not real."
