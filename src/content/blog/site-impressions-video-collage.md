The site for my ARCH201 studio at Pratt this semester is the MET Cloisters Museum in NYC. We are working on extracting aesthetic and contextual influence from the context of the site through video based analysis and collage. This video is a sample experience I created to demonstrate techniques and ways of seeing through video (AfterEffects).
